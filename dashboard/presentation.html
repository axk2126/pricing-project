Report part 4 · Presentation and constraints

Show the saving. Say why there is a fee.

The value message, the comparison the website shows, how the prices are framed, and how each EU rule named in the case is met.

The value message

Value communication raises what less informed customers are willing to pay towards the economic value (Nagle & Müller 2018, Ch. 3). Here the gap is large: travellers perceive about €8.00 of a plan worth €12.00.

Headline · 8 words“No monthly fee. Pay only for what you use.”

Says the two things a student needs to hear: the account stays at €0, and there is no hidden charge for holding it.

Value sentence under “Pricing”“Keep the account at €0 — add Plus or Travel when you need more.”

Presents the plans as something added, never as a way to avoid a charge.

Message strategyEconomic value communication

Fees can be compared before buying (a search good) and the benefit is money. So the page shows numbers: what a typical travelling month costs here and elsewhere.

The comparison shown on the website

What a typical travelling month costs: €800 of card payments, €400 of them in other currencies, two withdrawals abroad, travel insurance

DecisionThe page shows this comparison as a table under the plans, with the usage it assumes and the note that the customer’s own usage may differ. A calculator next to it lets visitors enter their own withdrawals and currency payments and see which offer is cheapest for them.

Prices of the alternatives are the competitor facts of the case. They are current prices of other offers, not earlier prices of Novčić, so nothing is presented as a reduction.

Price presentation: five effects, used responsibly

Effect (Nagle & Müller, Ch. 3)On the pageWhy, and where the line is
Reference priceThe comparison table sets the reference at what the same month costs elsewhere (€12–23), not at last year’s €0.After a free year the natural reference price is zero. Showing the real alternatives is the honest way to move it.
Order of presentationTravel is the highlighted card.A higher price seen as the featured option raises the reference for Plus. The cards still run Basic → Plus → Travel so the €0 account is seen first.
Gain–loss framingPlans are “added”; the withdrawal fee is the price of a service.A surcharge on a free account would feel like a loss. A priced service with a stated cost does not.
FairnessThe reason for the fees is said openly: cash and currency payments cost money, and those who use them pay.Buyers accept prices linked to costs and resent prices raised because the seller can.
Expenditure effectPrices are per month and stay that way.€6.99 a month is already a small expenditure; restating it per day would add nothing but a trick.

The constraints of the case, one by one

RuleWhat it requiresHow the price list and the website meet it
Fee information document and yearly statement
Payment Accounts Directive 2014/92/EU, Art. 4–5		Every fee listed under a standard name before the account is opened; once a year a statement of all fees paid.The price list has five fees in total, each with a standard name: monthly plan fee (Plus, Travel), cash withdrawal, currency conversion. The website says the document is received before opening (How it works, FAQ).
Currency conversion charge
Regulation (EU) 2021/1230, Art. 4		Shown as a percentage above the latest ECB reference rate, before the payment.The fee is defined in exactly that form: “1.5% above the ECB rate”, 0% in Travel. No flat currency fee, no hidden exchange-rate margin.
“Free”
Unfair Commercial Practices Directive 2005/29/EC, Annex I, No 20		An offer may be called free only if the customer pays nothing.Basic is “€0.00 per month” and “no monthly fee”, never “free”: fees per use apply. The FAQ says so in its first answer.
VAT
VAT Directive 2006/112/EC, Art. 135(1)(d)		Account fees are exempt.Price note under the plans: “Account fees are exempt from VAT; all prices are final.”
No fake reductions
project brief		No “was” prices or invented discounts.The only lower price is the annual Travel plan at €69.90, the price of ten months, for a twelve-month commitment.

The interchange income of 0.2% is itself a legal limit (Regulation (EU) 2015/751, Art. 3), which is why it cannot be raised and appears as a what-if on the risk page.

Introducing fees after a free year

The first price becomes the reference for every later one

  • The account keeps its price. What customers had for a year, the account and the card at €0, does not change. Only services with a cost get a price.
  • No introductory discount. A cheaper first year would make the full price feel like an increase at renewal.
  • Notice and a reason. Customers get the fee information document two months before 1 January 2027, with the reason stated: a withdrawal costs Novčić €1.00; the fee is €1.50.
  • A choice instead of a surcharge. Anyone who withdraws often or pays abroad is shown the plan that makes it cheaper.

DecisionLater increases follow the same rule: only when a cost rises, by the amount it rose, and said in those words.
