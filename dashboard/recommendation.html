Final recommendation

Basic €0 · Plus €3.99 · Travel €6.99.

A contribution of €876,182 against €700,000 of fixed costs, 94.6% of customers kept, and a structure whose per-use fees can absorb the shocks the case is likely to ask about.

Plans are monthly, cancel any time. Account fees are exempt from VAT; all prices are final. Basic is never called "free": it has no monthly fee, but per-use fees apply.

Why this and not the alternatives

Because the account has no value to price

Students are 60% of the base and their next-best alternative costs about nothing. A monthly fee loses 41.5% of all customers. Basic at €0 is the price of keeping the generation.

Because cash and currencies are the costs

Per-use fees above cost turn every segment positive and lose 5.4% of customers. They are also the two levers that can follow a cost or regulatory shock without touching the account.

Because the plans are chosen, not imposed

Plus at €3.99 and Travel at €6.99 earn €196k on top of per-use fees, keep 94.6% of the customers and pass every breakeven test against the neighbouring price points.

Implementation

  1. November 2026 · fee information document with every fee under its standard name; support staff certified; price page live with the ECB mark-up shown before payment.
  2. December 2026 · two months’ notice to all 40,000 customers: the account stays at €0, cash and currency payments are priced at their cost plus a margin, Plus and Travel available from 1 January.
  3. 1 January 2027 · price list in force. No introductory discount; the annual Travel option is the only commitment discount.
  4. Monthly · take-up and churn by segment, withdrawals per Basic customer, cash cost coverage.
  5. Quarterly · scorecard review; price moves only on evidence: withdrawal fee if cost coverage falls below 100%, Travel if take-up at €6.99 exceeds 80% for two quarters (then test €7.99).
