Reading

Total contribution €-580,800 a year; with fixed costs a loss of €-1,280,800. Cash is the largest leak: 56,000 withdrawals a month cost €672,000 a year, more than the €480,000 earned from card payments. Any price list that leaves cash free leaves the company in loss.

DecisionCharge for cash and currencies per use; they are the two costs that vary with behaviour. The €0.60 account cost is covered by interchange for employees and travellers, and nearly for students.

What they would pay for a plan

Students · Plus features

Free withdrawals, shared accounts, virtual cards. Thin demand: the plan is a nice-to-have for students.

Employees · Plus features

Half would pay €2.99, 40% €3.99, 20% €4.99. The ladder drops sharply above €3.99.

Travellers · Travel features

80% at €5.99, 70% at €6.99, 30% at €8.99: the cliff between €6.99 and €8.99 sets the price.

Reading

The survey is the willingness-to-pay evidence the course asks for (handout 07: estimates of WTP fit when customers understand the product). The chosen price in each ladder is highlighted; the price grid on the scenario page shows why.

How they react to fees: the number that decides the structure

Customers lost under each kind of fee

DecisionA monthly fee loses 41.5% of the base, per-use fees 5.4%. That is the single most important number in the case: it rules out a flat fee and makes the per-use account the floor of the structure.

Monthly fee: 55% of students, 25% of employees, 10% of travellers leave. Per-use fees: 5% / 3% / 70% of those who pay them; plan buyers do not pay them, so only non-buyers are exposed.

What 41.5% means for the company

  • 23,400 customers remain. With everything included, the flat fee that breaks even on them is €3.74; at €3.99 the company would make €70k: a smaller but profitable company.
  • It is a floor, not a curve. The survey did not ask at which fee people leave, so above about €4 the figure is not usable (only 4% of students would pay €4.99 for a richer plan).
  • It throws away the pipeline. A retained student is worth €11 a year today, an employee €38. 13,200 of the leavers are the future employee base.
  • It is avoidable. The recommended structure keeps 37,840 customers and earns €106k more.

DecisionTreat retention of the student segment as a strategic objective, not a cost. The €0 account is the price of the pipeline.
