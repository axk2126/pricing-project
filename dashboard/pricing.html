Pricing strategy · structure, level, presentation

Three offers, two per-use fees, no monthly fee.

The structure follows the strategic pricing pyramid: value first, then metrics and fences, then the level inside the value range, then how the price is shown.

Plans are monthly, cancel any time. Account fees are exempt from VAT; all prices are final. Basic is never called "free": it has no monthly fee, but per-use fees apply.

Floor, ceiling, chosen price (handouts 03 and 07)

Value range and the price inside it

DecisionPlus neutral at the 50:50 point of its range; Travel penetration at 58% of the reference value, because the survey cliff between €6.99 and €8.99 is steeper than the margin gain.

Basic has no range: the account’s reference value for a student is close to €0, so the monthly fee is €0 and the money is earned per use.

DecisionVersions do the segmenting; customers self-select. Two real fences: the cap of 3 included withdrawals in Plus (quantity fence) and, if ever used, student verification for a student price (buyer identification). The student price is worth only €8k a year and is not used.

ConceptWhere it is usedWhy
AnchoringFluxa Premium at €9.99 is the visible anchor in the market; Travel at €6.99 sits under it with insurance included; the €12.00 comparison is shown next to the pricethe reference price customers bring is the Fluxa ladder; anchor against it, do not fight it
Tiered pricing (good–better–best)Basic → Plus → Travel, each including the one below; Travel highlighted as recommendedself-selection with little enforcement; the recommended tier draws attention to the highest-margin plan
Willingness to paysurvey ladders at three price points per segment; prices chosen on the ladder, not between pointsno invented demand; every price is one customers were asked about
Value communicationeconomic value communication: numbers, a comparison table, one headline of 8 wordsfees are a search good with an economic benefit; show the saving, do not describe it
Price presentation€x.99 prices, per month, no ‘free’, no ‘was’ prices, VAT note, fees under standard names, ECB mark-up before paymentcharm prices in a category where they are the norm; every legal constraint of the case met on the page
Gain–loss framingplans are added benefits; the withdrawal fee is the price of a service linked to its costa surcharge feels like a loss; a priced service with a stated cost feels fair
