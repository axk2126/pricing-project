Workbook · Alternatives

One monthly fee for everyone: why the best-looking alternative is not credible

Profit of one fee at each level, with the survey churn held constant

DecisionHeld constant, the survey churn makes a €4.99 fee look better than the recommendation (€351k). That cannot be right: it assumes 45% of students stay at a mandatory €4.99, although only 4% would pay €4.99 for a plan with extra features. In the credible range up to €3.99 one fee earns at most €70k and loses 16,600 customers.

Everything included, no fees per use. The fee that breaks even on the 23,400 remaining customers is €3.74.

Workbook · Break-even

Without the plans there is no break-even

Fees per use alone (B) end at −€19,456. The two plans add €195,638: €75,600 from Travel, which also keeps 1,960 travellers who would otherwise leave, and €120,038 from Plus.

For each price the breakeven sales change of a move to the neighbouring surveyed price is on page 2.

DecisionThe plans are what turns a break-even price list into a profitable one.
