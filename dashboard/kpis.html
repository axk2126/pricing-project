Mission, vision, strategy

Mission

Give every 18–26-year-old in Croatia a euro account that costs nothing to hold and only charges for what costs us money.

Vision

The first account of a generation — and still their account at 27.

Value proposition

Operational excellence (Treacy & Wiersema): no monthly fee, low friction, a price list readable in one minute. Not product leadership, not the widest range.

Strategy

Win and keep the 18–26 segment with a €0 account, make every variable cost visible and paid for by the customer who causes it, and earn the margin from two plans that customers choose for themselves. The mission and vision are drafted for the project; the case sheet does not state them.

Why eight

Fewer, meaningful KPIs create clarity; too many KPIs disconnected from strategy cause confusion and gaming. Five are lead indicators that can still be acted on in 2027; three are lag indicators that confirm the strategy worked. Each has a named owner and is reviewed quarterly next to the monthly financial review.
