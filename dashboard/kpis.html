Roll-out

  1. November 2026. Fee information document with every fee under its standard name; support staff certified; price page live.
  2. December 2026. Two months’ notice to all 40,000 customers: the account stays at €0, cash and currency payments get a price, Plus and Travel can be chosen from 1 January.
  3. 1 January 2027. Price list in force. No introductory discount and no yearly price.
  4. Every month. Take-up and churn by segment, withdrawals per Basic customer, withdrawal fees against withdrawal cost.
  5. Every quarter. Scorecard review. Prices move only on evidence, by the rules below.
