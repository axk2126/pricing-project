Security and regulation

How your money is protected

Novčić Pay is a regulated financial institution. These are the rules we work under, in plain words.

Licensed and supervised

Novčić Pay d.o.o., Zagreb, is a licensed e-money institution and works under the supervision of the Croatian National Bank.

Your money is kept separate

Customer funds are safeguarded: held apart from our own money in dedicated accounts at EU credit institutions, as the law requires. We do not lend them out.

Clear about what we are

We are an e-money institution, not a bank. The deposit guarantee scheme therefore does not apply; your funds are protected by the safeguarding described here.

Two-step confirmation

Sign-in and payments are confirmed with two independent factors, and online card payments with 3-D Secure, as EU payment rules require.

Every fee in writing

A fee information document before you open the account, the currency mark-up shown before you confirm a payment, and a statement of all fees once a year.

Support and complaints

Write to us and a person answers. A formal complaint receives a written reply within 15 working days.

FAQ

Questions

What people ask before they open the account.

Is my money safe with Novčić Pay?

Novčić Pay is a licensed e-money institution supervised by the Croatian National Bank. Your money is safeguarded: it is held separately from our own funds in dedicated accounts at EU credit institutions and is not lent out.

Is Novčić Pay a bank?

No. We are an e-money institution. You get a euro account and a debit card, but we do not grant loans and the deposit guarantee scheme for bank deposits does not apply. Your funds are protected by safeguarding instead.

Is the Basic account free?

The Basic account has no monthly fee. You pay €1.50 for each cash withdrawal and 1.5% above the ECB reference rate on payments in other currencies, so we do not call it free. Everything else in Basic is included.

How do I see the fees before I pay?

Before you open the account you receive a fee information document that lists every fee under its standard name. In the app, the currency conversion fee is shown as a percentage above the ECB reference rate before you confirm a payment. Once a year you receive a statement of all fees you paid.

Can I switch between Basic, Plus and Travel?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled at the end of any month; you then return to Basic.

How do the included cash withdrawals work?

In Plus, the first three cash withdrawals in a calendar month are included. In Travel, the first five are included, anywhere in the world. After that, each withdrawal costs €1.50, as in Basic.
