Benefits

Why Novčić Pay

Built for the way people aged 18 to 26 actually use money: a card for everyday payments, a quick way to split a bill, and a place to save for the next trip.

Nothing to pay for holding the account

The account and the debit card cost €0 a month. You pay only for cash withdrawals and for payments in other currencies, and every fee is shown before you confirm.

Instant transfers to friends

Split the dinner, the rent or the festival tickets in seconds. Money arrives instantly between Novčić Pay accounts.

Savings goals that stick

Set a goal, put money aside automatically, and watch it grow. The plans add shared accounts, virtual cards and a travel package when you need them.

Three steps

How it works

1

Open the account in the app

Confirm your identity with your ID card. Before you open it, you receive the fee information document that lists every fee.

2

Get your euro account and card

Your account is ready at once; the debit card follows by post. Use the virtual card in the meantime if you choose Plus or Travel.

3

Pay, transfer and save

Pay with the card, send money to friends instantly and set your savings goals. Add Plus or Travel at any time, and cancel monthly.

FAQ

Questions

Four things people ask before they open the account.

Is the Basic account free?

The Basic account has no monthly fee. You pay €1.50 for each cash withdrawal and 1.5% above the ECB reference rate on payments in other currencies, so we do not call it free. Everything else in Basic is included.

How do I see the fees before I pay?

Before you open the account you receive a fee information document that lists every fee under its standard name. In the app, the currency conversion fee is shown as a percentage above the ECB reference rate before you confirm a payment. Once a year you receive a statement of all fees you paid.

Can I switch between Basic, Plus and Travel?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled at the end of any month; you then return to Basic. If you choose the annual Travel plan, it runs for twelve months at €69.90.

What happens to the 3 included withdrawals in Plus?

The first three cash withdrawals in a calendar month are included in Plus. From the fourth withdrawal the Basic fee of €1.50 applies. In Travel, cash withdrawals worldwide are included.
